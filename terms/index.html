Terms of Service — Missed-call text notifications

Program

Rob's Automation Services operates a transactional missed-call text notification program. If you call +17754068509 and the call is not answered, we may send one SMS so you can reply with what you need.

Who sends the messages

Messages are sent only by Rob's Automation Services. This is not affiliate marketing, lead generation for other companies, or third-party promotional messaging.

Consent

By texting START to +17754068509, or by calling +17754068509 after being informed that a missed call may result in a transactional SMS, you agree to receive transactional text messages from Rob's Automation Services about that missed call and related follow-up for the same request.

Consent is given directly to Rob's Automation Services. Consent is not shared with other brands.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency: one SMS per missed call, and no more than one message every 2 hours to the same number.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. After STOP you will receive no further program messages unless you opt in again.

No third-party sharing of opt-in data

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties, affiliates, or partners for their marketing or promotional purposes.
