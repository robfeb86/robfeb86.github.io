Terms of Service — Missed-call text notifications

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Program

Rob's Automation Services operates a transactional missed-call text notification program under the brand name Rob's Automation Services.

If you call +17754068509 and the call is not answered, we may send one SMS from Rob's Automation Services so you can reply with what you need. These messages are transactional notices about that missed call, not advertising from other companies.

Who sends the messages

Messages come from Rob's Automation Services only. We send SMS solely for our own missed-call notification program.

This is not third-party marketing, affiliate marketing, lead generation for other companies, or promotional messaging on behalf of any other brand. No other business sends texts under this program.

Consent

By texting START to +17754068509, or by calling +17754068509 after being informed that a missed call may result in a transactional SMS, you agree to receive transactional text messages from Rob's Automation Services about that missed call and related follow-up for the same request.

Consent is given directly to Rob's Automation Services for this program only. Consent is campaign-specific and is not bundled into other agreements. Consent is not transferred, sold, or shared with other brands.

Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency: one SMS per missed call, and no more than one message every 2 hours to the same number.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. After STOP you will receive no further program messages unless you opt in again. You can still call the business directly.

Customer support

For help, reply HELP to any message from this program, or call +17754068509. For questions, contact the business that called you back or the operator of this number.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
