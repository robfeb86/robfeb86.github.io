Privacy Policy — Missed-call text notifications

We use your mobile number only to send a transactional text after we miss your phone call, and to continue that same conversation if you reply.

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers with third parties for their marketing.

Message frequency: one SMS per missed call, and no more than one message every 2 hours to the same number.

Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help.

For questions, contact the business that called you back or email the operator of this number.
